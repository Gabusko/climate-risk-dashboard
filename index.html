        How farmers get the same warning

        No app or smartphone needed. Farmers receive a Spanish text message and reply with one number. Their replies come back to the dashboard, so buyers and exporters see what is happening on the ground.

        Try it: tap a reply on the phone. Support requests start a verified assessment; they do not trigger automatic payouts, loans or supplier exclusion.

        Data sources in the full concept: IDEAM daily landslide-threat bulletins, NASA POWER recent conditions (2–3 day delay), Copernicus seasonal forecasts (monthly), NOAA CPC ENSO outlooks, plus supplier harvest, temperature and arrival-inspection records.

        All regions, shipments, figures and messages on this page are illustrative for a student pitch. Risk scores would be reviewed by agronomists, and missing data is shown as unknown rather than guessed. Not every flood or landslide is caused by El Niño.
